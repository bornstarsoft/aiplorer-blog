# Aiplorer Practical Workflow Guides

Date: 2026-07-26

## Purpose

Four reviewed guides expand Aiplorer's task-first discovery layer:

- AI automation tools
- AI coding tools
- multimodal creative workflows
- AI meeting tools

The guides turn strong reviewed-tool categories into practical entry points.
Each page defines the work, review checkpoints, comparison criteria, and a
content-specific handoff to the AI Tool Decision Path.

## Trend rationale

The batch follows current official research that describes AI work moving
toward repeatable, longer, multi-step workflows while keeping people
responsible for goals, quality standards, permissions, handoffs, and outcomes.

Sources checked:

- Microsoft 2026 Work Trend Index:
  <https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/agents-human-agency-and-the-opportunity-for-every-organization>
- OpenAI, How agents are transforming work:
  <https://openai.com/index/how-agents-are-transforming-work/>

## Discovery behavior

- Automation guide opens the `automation-agents` Decision Path with
  `reliability`.
- Coding guide opens `coding-build` with `reliability`.
- Creative guide opens `multimodal-create` with `rights`.
- Meeting guide opens `meetings-knowledge` with `privacy`.
- Related reviewed tool cards come from existing front matter and remain
  alphabetical.

## Editorial boundary

- The guides do not rank, rate, score, or endorse tools.
- No exact pricing, limits, product availability, security guarantee, license
  guarantee, or feature claim was added.
- Readers are reminded to verify current official product details.
- No tool page or draft status changed.
- Existing `/posts/<slug>/` URLs remain outside this work.
