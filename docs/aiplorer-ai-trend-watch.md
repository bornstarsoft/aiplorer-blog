# Aiplorer AI Trend Watch

## Purpose

`/ai-tools/trend-watch/` gives returning visitors a compact, source-backed view
of current AI workflow changes. It connects each signal to an existing
Aiplorer workflow collection so trend coverage improves discovery instead of
becoming a news feed or a thin link directory.

## Editorial boundary

- Signals are editorial interpretations, not predictions, rankings, ratings,
  endorsements, financial advice, or guarantees.
- Product-level claims remain on reviewed tool pages and must continue to use
  official product sources.
- Trend signals must be dated, link to the original source, and include a
  practical review checkpoint.
- A trend does not make a tool suitable, accurate, safe, or available for every
  user.

## Maintenance

Review the signals as a small editorial set rather than adding every industry
headline. Update the checked date only after re-reading the linked official
sources. Replace or retire a signal when it no longer helps visitors choose a
workflow. Keep the page lightweight, server-rendered, and free of popularity
scores or automated trend scraping.
