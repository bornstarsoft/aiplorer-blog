# Aiplorer Guides and Use Cases Refresh

Date: 2026-07-26

## Purpose

The Guides and Use Cases routes now connect practical reading to the existing
Aiplorer discovery workflow. Visitors can move from a task, to a review
checkpoint, to the AI Tool Decision Path, reviewed tool pages, Shortlist, and
comparison view without changing the existing public URLs.

## Experience

- Section pages use the same task-first visual language as the AI discovery
  area.
- Individual guides and use cases show their audience, review date, and a
  compact table of contents.
- Each article provides a content-specific Decision Path handoff.
- Reviewed related tools are resolved from existing front matter and shown
  alphabetically as context, not as rankings or endorsements.
- Product details, pricing, limits, policies, and availability still require
  verification at the official source.

## Trend rationale

Current official research emphasizes repeatable workflows, clear goals,
documented human handoffs, and human responsibility as AI and agents take on
more execution. Aiplorer therefore treats guides as workflow entry points
rather than isolated articles or recommendation lists.

Sources checked:

- Microsoft 2026 Work Trend Index:

## Boundaries

- No rankings, ratings, scores, paid placement, account, or tracking was added.
- No tool page or draft status changed.
- Existing `/guides/`, `/use-cases/`, and article URLs remain unchanged.
- Legacy `/posts/<slug>/` routes are outside this change.
