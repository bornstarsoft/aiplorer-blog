## Source Freshness Review

Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Pitch, and SlidesAI were rechecked against current
official product, pricing, help, privacy, security, and policy sources on
2026-07-25. All four remain published with cautious high-level descriptions,
updated source notes, and refreshed review dates.

Gamma now presents a broader visual-content product spanning presentations,
documents, websites, graphics, social content, and programmatic creation. Its
page remains presentation-focused while adding cautions around workspace
permissions, sharing visibility, connected services, and AI data-use settings.

Beautiful.ai remains a presentation platform with AI-assisted workflows,
Smart Slides, templates, brand controls, collaboration, and sharing. Its page
adds cautions around cloud storage, account and workspace policies, source
material, internal support access, and third-party AI provider terms without
making privacy or security guarantees.

Pitch remains a collaborative presentation workspace and now documents Pitch
Agent, richer external sharing, and visitor analytics. Its page adds cautions
around workspace AI controls, link access, analytics, visitor consent, and
privacy-notice obligations.

SlidesAI now documents presentation workflows across its web app, Google
Slides, PowerPoint, and a ChatGPT-related path. Its page adds cautions around
account permissions, connected presentation services, uploaded source
material, and third-party processing.

The refreshed pages continue to avoid exact pricing, credits, input or slide
limits, plan comparisons, model claims, generation-quality claims, export
guarantees, template or language counts, collaboration guarantees,
commercial-use guarantees, privacy or security guarantees, and exact feature
availability.
