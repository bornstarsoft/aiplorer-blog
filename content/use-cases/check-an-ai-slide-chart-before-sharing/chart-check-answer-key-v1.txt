Keep separate from the test conversation. These are editorial expectations.

V-01: 12 of 20 respondents reported completing onboarding (60%).
Use respondent counts and label the base as 20. The 50 invitees are context,
not the denominator of the 60% statistic. Do not assign a completion status
to the 30 people who did not respond or generalize to all invitees.

V-02: Completion rose from 40% in A to 50% in B, up 10 percentage points.
Label the periods and the completion share; the base is 100 in each period.
A 25% relative increase is correct if labeled, but not a substitute for the
requested percentage-point statement. Do not infer a causal effect.

V-03: Requests recorded: 24 in October and 30 in November.
Show the two observed counts, labeled by month. Omit December from the
observed series, or note that it was not measured. Do not plot 36 or zero
as a December observation.

Accept equivalent wording only when the displayed numbers, labels and
limitations agree. A good text review is not an export or rendering test.
