## Compare The Quote With The Source

A quote can sound plausible and still put the wrong words in someone's mouth. Compare three draft quotes with a fictional workshop transcript before adding them to an event recap.

## Check Three Quotes

| Case | Look for | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QUOTE-01 | A rewritten statement in quotation marks | Match the transcript verbatim or label the rewrite as a paraphrase. |
| QUOTE-02 | The correct words under the wrong name | Match the quote to the speaker who actually said it. |
| QUOTE-03 | A shortened excerpt that drops a condition | Keep the condition so the quote does not imply an unconditional commitment. |

Read the whole line and nearby context before using a short excerpt. For real material, confirm the transcript and permission to quote before publishing.

## Retest With The Same Excerpt

Reuse the same transcript and draft quotes. Compare which errors each run identifies and whether the corrected quotes match their sources.
