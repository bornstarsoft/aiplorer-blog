## Keep Effort, Scope, And Dates Separate

An estimate can add up and still mislead. In this exercise, check an AI-drafted estimate for a fictional workshop handout against the assumptions the team actually agreed to.

## Check Three Claims

| Case | Look for | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EST-01 | Work hours reported as time until delivery | Preserve the effort unit. Check staff availability and review timing before estimating elapsed time. |
| EST-02 | Optional work included in the base scope | Show the base estimate separately from work that needs approval. |
| EST-03 | A date that overlooks a dependency | Name what must be received or confirmed before promising the date. |

Before sharing a real estimate, distinguish a planning assumption from a commitment. If a date or approval remains open, keep it open in the summary.
