## Would a Reader Take the Right Action?

An announcement can sound ready to publish and still send people to the wrong room or promise a seat that is not available. Compare a fictional workshop announcement with the organizer's confirmed brief, then revise only what the brief supports.

## Get the Pack

Give the AI only the test input. Use the separate key to check its first answer and the worksheet to compare a second run.

| Case | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| EVENT-01 | Use the current room, not the room listed in the earlier draft. |
| EVENT-02 | Distinguish joining a waitlist from securing a seat. |
| EVENT-03 | Do not promise a replay when the organizer has not confirmed one. |

Before publishing a real announcement, confirm any changed details with the organizer and test the registration link as a visitor.
