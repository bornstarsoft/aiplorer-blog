## Review What Each Shot Implies

A product video can make a claim with a button, an edit or a sound even
when the narration is accurate. Compare each shot with what the brief
actually approves, including where the finished video will be used.

This text-only exercise uses a fictional product brief and draft shots.
It does not use actual video, music or model output.

## Get The Pack

## Check Three Review Boundaries

| Case | What the draft implies | What to retain or change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SHOT-01 | An Export button is part of the product. | Use the approved list and Status filter. Remove the invented control and export claim. |
| SHOT-02 | A processing step finishes instantly. | Remove the speed claim. If the cut is retained, label the sequence as shortened. |
| SHOT-03 | The private-review soundtrack can stay in the public release. | Use silence for this exercise. Keep the approved title card and do not carry the placeholder audio into the public version. |

Check the visual evidence, caption and audio separately. For SHOT-02,
deleting the caption alone would still leave an unlabeled jump cut. A
review should address what a viewer could infer from the finished sequence.

## Compare A Later Run

Save the first proposal and the same brief. Compare which controls, edits
and assets the next response accepts or rejects. Record missing evidence
instead of turning it into a product or rights claim.
