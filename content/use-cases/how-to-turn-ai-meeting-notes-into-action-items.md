## Goal

Turn permitted meeting notes or a transcript into a short, reviewed record of
decisions, action owners, due dates, open questions, and follow-up without
treating an AI summary as the authoritative record.

Confirm consent, recording rules, workplace policy, client expectations, and
applicable law before recording, transcribing, or processing a conversation.

## Define The Output

Decide what the team needs:

- confirmed decisions
- action, owner, and due date
- open questions
- risks or blockers
- items that need approval
- a short follow-up message

Do not request a broad summary when the useful outcome is a precise action
record.

## Build The Follow-Up Workflow

1. Confirm permission and purpose.
   Make sure required participants understand the recording, transcription, or
   AI assistance and how the result will be used.

2. Limit the source material.
   Use only the approved notes or transcript. Remove unrelated sensitive
   details before further processing where appropriate.

3. Request a structured draft.
   Ask for decisions, actions, owners, dates, unresolved questions, and items
   that the notes do not establish clearly.

4. Compare against the source.
   Check names, speakers, numbers, dates, conditions, commitments, negation,
   and context in the original conversation or approved notes.

5. Confirm ownership.
   Ask each responsible person to confirm their action and due date. Do not let
   the AI assign commitments that were not agreed.

6. Separate facts from suggestions.
   Mark proposed next steps as proposals rather than decisions.

7. Share only with the right people.
   Review recipients, permissions, links, attachments, and whether the raw
   transcript should be included.

8. Apply the retention plan.
   Keep or delete recordings, transcripts, summaries, and drafts according to
   the approved policy and current account controls.

## Use A Simple Action Format

For each item, record:

- **Action:** the specific next step
- **Owner:** the person who confirmed responsibility
- **Due date:** the agreed date or "not decided"
- **Source:** where the decision appears in the approved notes
- **Status:** proposed, confirmed, blocked, or complete

This format makes missing ownership and unsupported deadlines visible.

## Review Sensitive Meetings Carefully

Legal, medical, financial, contractual, personnel, investigation, security,
customer, and business-critical meetings require extra care and may not be
suitable for recording or AI processing.

AI notes can omit conditions or misattribute statements. Keep important
records in the approved system and involve a qualified professional where
required.

## Next Steps

Test the action-item format on a low-risk meeting with clear permission. Ask
owners to confirm every commitment, then review whether the resulting record
is shorter, clearer, and safer to share than the raw AI output.
