## Check The Story Before Designing Slides

An outline can look ready for slides while telling the wrong story. In this exercise, review three proposed slides for a fictional internal pilot. Use the brief to separate what the team knows, what it plans to measure, and what the audience can approve.

## Check Three Slides

| Case | Compare | What to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SLIDE-01 | Opening slide and intended audience | Explain the internal handoff problem to the people deciding whether to test a change. |
| SLIDE-02 | Results claim and available evidence | Describe a planned measurement as a plan, not a demonstrated improvement. |
| SLIDE-03 | Closing slide and decision scope | Ask for the limited pilot. Keep a broader rollout outside this decision. |

Rewrite only what the brief supports. Mark missing evidence instead of filling it with a plausible result.

## Retest With The Same Brief

Compare which draft catches each mismatch before you invest time in slide design. For a real deck, confirm the audience, evidence, and approval scope with the owner.
