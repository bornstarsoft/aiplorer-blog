## Separate An Action From A Suggestion

A neat action list can conceal the difference between volunteering,
accepting responsibility, and reviewing someone else's work. Check the
final decision for each item and keep its owner and status distinct.

This text-only exercise uses fictional notes and drafts, not a recorded
meeting or an observed AI result. Keep it out of connected task systems.

## Review Three Decision Boundaries

| Case | Draft error | What the meeting supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACT-01 | A volunteered export becomes an assigned Friday task. | The export is still proposed, subject to approval. No owner or deadline was confirmed. |
| ACT-02 | An earlier request appears as an open action after it was canceled. | Record the cancellation, not a pending task or a replacement. |
| ACT-03 | A reviewer becomes a co-owner and a draft becomes a send commitment. | Sol owns the draft due November 13. Ari is the reviewer. No send date was agreed. |

Keep canceled actions out of the open list, but retain the decision that
explains why they were removed. Where a reviewer and an owner are both
named, preserve their separate roles.

## Compare A Later Run

Repeat the same notes and instructions after a meaningful change. Compare
the status, scope, ownership and dates in both original answers before
asking for corrections. Start a baseline if you do not have an earlier
comparable result.
