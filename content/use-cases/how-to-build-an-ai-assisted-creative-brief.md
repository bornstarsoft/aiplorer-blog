## Goal

Turn a rough content or campaign idea into a one-page creative brief that a
person can review before any image, video, audio, slide, or public copy is
generated.

The brief should define the audience, message, factual sources, required media,
brand boundaries, source rights, consent needs, output specifications, and
approval owner.

## Gather The Inputs

Collect only approved material:

- the purpose and intended audience
- the primary message and call to action
- verified facts, product details, and source links
- required formats, dimensions, duration, and channels
- approved logos, colors, terminology, and accessibility requirements
- owned or licensed text, images, audio, footage, and other assets
- consent records for relevant voices, faces, or likenesses
- the person who approves the final work

Do not upload confidential strategy, unreleased products, customer data, or
third-party material without appropriate permission and controls.

## Draft The Brief

1. Write the objective.
   Describe what the audience should understand or do after seeing the work.

2. Define the audience and context.
   Note the channel, viewing situation, language, accessibility needs, and
   expected level of knowledge.

3. State the facts that must remain accurate.
   Include source links for names, dates, numbers, product claims, quotations,
   and other important details.

4. Define the media stages.
   Mark where you need an outline, visual concept, storyboard, draft image,
   rough audio, video edit, presentation structure, or final export.

5. Set brand and tone boundaries.
   Record approved terminology, visual rules, prohibited claims, and examples
   that should not be imitated.

6. Record rights and consent.
   Identify the owner and permitted use of every source asset, voice, face,
   likeness, trademark, music track, and reference.

7. Add review gates.
   Require separate approval for the concept, factual draft, media draft, and
   final publishing file where the risk warrants it.

## Review The AI Draft

Check:

- invented facts, text, objects, people, or citations
- inaccurate logos, product details, names, or captions
- unwanted bias, stereotypes, or deceptive representation
- accessibility, readability, contrast, captions, and audio clarity
- visible artifacts across the entire image, frame, slide, or recording
- source rights, consent, attribution, disclosure, and commercial-use needs
- final output in the real channel and device context

AI-generated media should remain a draft until these checks are complete.

## Keep Rights And Publishing Visible

Tool access does not establish permission to publish. Review current official
terms and the rights attached to source material and generated output.

Public, political, medical, financial, legal, reputation-sensitive, or
commercial communication may require additional professional, brand, legal,
or compliance review.
