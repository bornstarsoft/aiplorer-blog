## Choose A Return Path

Use [Change Watch](/ai-tools/review-updates/#tool-changes) when you want to
review announcements for particular tools. Use the public RSS feed when you
prefer to collect these notices alongside your other reading.

Neither route is a complete or real-time record of vendor changes. Aiplorer
selects announcements and records when their official sources were checked.
An empty result is not proof that a tool has not changed.

## Add The Public Feed

Copy this address into your RSS reader's feed-subscription field:

```text
https://aiplorer.com/ai-tools/review-updates/index.xml
```

[Open the RSS feed](/ai-tools/review-updates/index.xml). A browser may show XML
or offer a download; that is the feed document, not an error page.
Your reader controls when it fetches the feed and whether it sends alerts.
Aiplorer does not send email or push notifications through this feed.

The feed contains the recorded announcements across covered tools. Saving a
tool or choosing a filter on Aiplorer does not create a personalized RSS feed.
Avoid adding private account details or credentials to the feed address.

## Read The Dates In Context

| Detail | What to use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Announcement date | The date or month attached to the recorded event, not necessarily when you received it |
| Source checked date | When Aiplorer reviewed the linked source, not a promise of continuous monitoring |
| Official source | The place to confirm the current scope, account eligibility and instructions |
| Check before use | A practical question to investigate before changing a working setup |

Month-only events deliberately omit an exact RSS publication date.
The reader may display them differently. A revised record can also appear
again; that is not necessarily a second announcement.

## Give Each Notice One Next Step

In a short catch-up session, choose one outcome for each relevant notice:

- **No action for this workflow:** record why the announced scope does not apply.
- **Small test needed:** keep one non-sensitive reference task and compare results.
- **Owner review needed:** pause before changing permissions, payments or shared workflows.

For example, imagine a notice about a changed export workflow. This is a
fictional situation, not a claim about a particular service. Check whether you
use that export and whether the notice applies to your account. If it does,
test one disposable sample before moving a whole project. If it does not,
keep the reason and continue with your existing setup.

Use the [small retest exercise](/use-cases/retest-a-saved-ai-tool-after-an-update/)
when you need an output check, or the
[connected-app permission checklist](/guides/review-connected-ai-app-permissions/)
when a notice concerns account access.

## Keep Reading State Separate From A Decision

Marking an item read in your RSS reader does not mark it read on Aiplorer.
Aiplorer's read checks, saved tools and remembered filters are local to the
browser; they are not synced with the feed or another device.

A read check records that you read the notice, not that a tool passed a test.
Leave unresolved notices unread and keep a brief, non-sensitive next step in
[your shortlist](/ai-tools/shortlist/). Keep full test evidence in an approved
location, not in browser notes.

Return to [Change Watch](/ai-tools/review-updates/#tool-changes) when you are
ready for the next check. There is no need to replace a working tool just
because an announcement is new.
