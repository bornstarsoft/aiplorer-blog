## First, Find Out What Actually Changes

A discontinued feature is not necessarily a closed service. Open the official
notice and separate the affected feature, account or region from the rest of
the product. Record any stated deadline and timezone exactly. If no deadline
is published, write "not announced"; do not invent one from the article date.

This is a preparation checklist, not a claim that every provider offers export,
refunds or migration. Use the options documented for your account. Aiplorer's
[recorded changes](/ai-tools/review-updates/#tool-changes) cover selected notices,
not every service or every change.

## Preserve The Work, Not Just The Chat

Start with the deliverable someone must still be able to use. A saved prompt
alone cannot restore a workflow that depends on missing files or permissions.

| Keep | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Final deliverables | Open the actual documents, code, images or audio outside the service |
| Inputs and instructions | Retain approved source material, constraints and reusable prompts |
| Sources and rights | Keep original references, licenses, attribution and consent records |
| Workflow notes | Record settings, dependencies, human corrections and approval steps |
| Access and ownership | Identify who owns the files and who may move or receive them |

Use approved storage and copy only material you are authorized to retain.
Do not collect credentials, confidential customer data or another person's
workspace merely to make a more complete backup. If a required item cannot
be exported, record the gap and contact the provider through its official
support channel rather than assuming a workaround is permitted.

## Test One Small Handoff Before Moving Everything

Choose a non-sensitive, representative sample and open it in the destination
environment. Check content, formatting, links, attachments and editability.
For code, run the project's tests; for media, inspect the actual output file;
for research, verify the original sources rather than keeping citations alone.

Do not delete the original just because a download completed. A successful
download does not prove that the file is complete or usable. Keep the original
until the responsible owner accepts the handoff and retention rules allow it.

## Worked Example: A Small Presentation Project

This fictional exercise is not a test of any named provider. Suppose a team
must preserve a five-slide proposal with a chart and speaker notes.

1. Inventory the deck, chart source, approved logo and review comments.
2. Use an available, permitted export and record the file format.
3. Have the recipient open it in the application they will actually use.
4. Change a heading and one chart value; check links, notes and line breaks.
5. If the notes are missing, record "handoff incomplete" and recover approved
   notes separately before accepting the transfer.

The acceptance test is whether the recipient can continue the work, not
whether the replacement service produces an impressive new preview.

## Keep A Short Exit Record

```text
Service / affected workflow:
Official notice URL and date checked:
Deadline and timezone, or "not announced":
Work owner and approved destination:
Files preserved / items still missing:
Sample opened and edited by:
Checks passed / failed / not run:
Access, billing or retention questions still open:
Next action and responsible person:
```

Store this record with the approved project material. A browser-only shortlist
note is useful as a reminder, but is not a secure archive or a synchronized
backup. Do not put secrets or private customer information into it.

## Finish The Transition Deliberately

After the work owner confirms the handoff, review old sharing links, connected
accounts and access permissions using the
[connected-app checklist](/guides/review-connected-ai-app-permissions/).
Check billing and data-retention instructions
on the provider's official pages. Removing an integration, deleting an account
and cancelling a subscription are different actions; verify what each one does
before making an irreversible change.

Keep unresolved questions visible. "Could not test" is more useful than an
unsupported claim that the migration is complete.

Return to [your saved tools](/ai-tools/shortlist/) for relevant announcement
checks, or use the [small retest exercise](/use-cases/retest-a-saved-ai-tool-after-an-update/)
when a service changes but your workflow remains available.
