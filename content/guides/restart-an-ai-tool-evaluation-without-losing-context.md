## Start With The Reason To Recheck

A new announcement does not mean every earlier check is invalid. Identify
what changed in the product or your own task, then choose the relevant
check. For example, a different input format may call for another output
test; changed account permissions need a separate permissions review.

Read the applicable official notice before assuming a change reaches your
account. Aiplorer records selected announcements, not a complete change
history for every service.

## Preserve The Previous Result First

Before clearing completed checks, keep a dated summary of what you actually
tested, what failed, and what remains unresolved. Store full evidence in
an approved location. A browser-local note is a short reminder, not an
evidence archive or a revision history.

For illustration only, an earlier note might say:

> Prior test: three invented request messages. IDs were retained, but a
> proposed date became a confirmed deadline. Do not use that output as an
> agreed work plan. Next: try an explicit missing-value instruction.

This is a fictional example, not a result from a named service. Your own
note should describe your actual run, not copy an example as evidence.

Use [a shortlist backup](/guides/move-your-ai-shortlist-to-another-browser/)
when you need a separate copy before editing. A backup may contain your
private notes; keep it in an approved location and do not publish it.
Restoring a backup replaces local evaluation data rather than merging a
history of tests.

## Clear Only What You Intend To Revisit

If just one question needs another look, uncheck that item. If you want
to revisit all four candidate checks, **Reset checks** asks for confirmation
and clears only that saved tool's checked boxes.

The reset preserves:

- The tool in your saved list and its decision stage.
- Its private note and previously recorded local test date.
- Other candidates and their checks.
- Announcement read status.

It does not archive the previous checked boxes or create a new test record.
Cancel leaves the checks unchanged. If the page cannot verify the reset
was saved, review the displayed state rather than assuming it succeeded.

## Keep Old Evidence Separate From The Next Result

The previous test date remains a previous test date. Change it only after
you actually run another test. A reset does not move a candidate out of
**Ready to decide**; choose **Testing** yourself when that matches your
current decision stage.

Do not re-check output, privacy or plan items just to make progress look
complete. Each check answers a different question. "Could not test" is a
useful observation when a required setting, account or input is unavailable.

For the fictional request exercise, the next note could record:

> Next run: use the same three invented messages and preserve the original
> result separately. Check whether proposed dates remain unconfirmed.
> Record the actual outcome and human corrections before changing the date.

## Make The Next Visit Specific

Return to [your shortlist](/ai-tools/shortlist/) with one unresolved question,
not a requirement to complete every box. Try the
[missing-value extraction exercise](/use-cases/test-ai-data-extraction-with-missing-values/)
or the [before-and-after retest](/use-cases/retest-a-saved-ai-tool-after-an-update/).
Checklists are private reminders, not scores or proof of general reliability.
