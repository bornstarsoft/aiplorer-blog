AI business tools can help with marketing ideas, customer support drafts, internal workflows, documents, and operational tasks. This category is for small business owners, freelancers, and teams exploring practical AI support.

Generated business content, customer-facing messages, automated actions, and workflow changes should be reviewed and tested before use. Protect customer, employee, account, store, order, and confidential business data. Check connected-account permissions, escalation and rollback steps, privacy requirements, legal and compliance obligations, brand guidance, and workplace policy.
