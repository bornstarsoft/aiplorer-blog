AI business tools can help with marketing ideas, customer support drafts, internal workflows, documents, and operational tasks. This category is for small business owners, freelancers, and teams exploring practical AI support.

Use [Airtable AI](/ai-tools/tools/airtable-ai/) to try tagging individual requests in a test queue. Use [Dovetail](/ai-tools/tools/dovetail/) when you need to read across feedback and verify which comments support a theme. In both cases, keep the AI suggestion separate from your team's final decision.

For recurring reporting, [Power BI Copilot](/ai-tools/tools/power-bi-copilot/) can help answer questions about an existing report, while [Tableau Pulse](/ai-tools/tools/tableau-pulse/) helps you follow a defined metric. Verify the source, filters, and comparison period before sharing a result.

Generated business content, customer-facing messages, automated actions, and workflow changes should be reviewed and tested before use. Protect customer, employee, account, store, order, and confidential business data. Check connected-account permissions, escalation and rollback steps, privacy requirements, legal and compliance obligations, brand guidance, and workplace policy.

Features, plans, pricing, limits, permissions, connected-product behavior, privacy terms, security details, and availability can change. Check each official website before choosing a tool or relying on a specific capability.
