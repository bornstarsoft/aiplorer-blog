Fin is an AI customer agent that may help teams prepare and manage customer-service, inbound sales, and commerce interactions for review. It may be useful for organizations exploring AI assistance for customer-facing content, workflows, actions, and escalation planning.

Teams may explore Fin for drafting support responses, preparing inbound sales conversation ideas, reviewing commerce guidance, or planning escalation and handoff workflows. Customer-facing answers, recommendations, and automated actions should be tested and reviewed before they affect a customer, prospect, account, order, or business process.
