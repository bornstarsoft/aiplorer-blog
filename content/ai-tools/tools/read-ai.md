## Overview

Read AI is a meeting productivity tool that may help users prepare transcript drafts, organize summaries, and review follow-up material from conversations. It can be useful when a person wants a first-pass meeting record before checking important details and the original discussion.

Read AI features, capture methods, plans, and policies may change. Users should check the official product, support, pricing, privacy, security, and legal pages before relying on a specific workflow.

## Useful For

Users may consider Read AI for preparing transcript drafts, organizing meeting summaries, reviewing decisions, or drafting follow-up questions and action items. Generated meeting material should be checked before it is shared, published, or entered into business systems.

Review transcripts, summaries, speaker labels, decisions, and action items before relying on them. Obtain any required participant consent and follow applicable recording laws and workplace policies.
