## What it is

HeyGen focuses on videos with a presenter, including avatar-based creation and translation workflows. Consider it for an explanation or training script rather than as a substitute for every kind of cinematic video generator.

## A useful first trial

Start with a short approved script that includes your product name and one sentence with numbers. Review the result with sound on and captions visible. Check pronunciation, the meaning of each claim, and whether the presentation could mislead a viewer about who actually recorded it.

For translated video, have someone fluent in the target language review the script and final output. A convincing voice does not establish that the translation is correct.
