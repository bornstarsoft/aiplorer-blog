## Test What the Other Person Hears

The [device guide](https://help.krisp.ai/hc/en-us/articles/4402174576402-How-Krisp-Microphone-and-Krisp-Speaker-work)
distinguishes outgoing microphone audio from incoming speaker audio. Check
the [setup requirements](https://help.krisp.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360017441080-Set-up-any-app-with-Krisp)
for your particular calling app. In an approved test call, ask a coworker
to listen as you read a short line with ambient noise nearby. Compare with
and without the filter. Check whether quiet speech or the ends of words are
missing, not just whether the background sounds quieter.

## Keep Recording and Noise Cancellation Separate

Krisp's [data-handling guide](https://help.krisp.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360012035500-What-data-Krisp-sends-to-the-cloud)
describes desktop noise cancellation on the device, separate from how meeting
assistant features process audio and notes. That distinction does not change
what your calling app transmits. For a noise-only trial, verify that no
recording or transcription is enabled in either app.

Recheck your audio device selection after changing rooms, headsets, or
calling apps. Review the current [plans](https://krisp.ai/pricing/) and
[privacy policy](https://krisp.ai/privacy-policy/) before relying on other
features. See the [meeting tools guide](/guides/how-to-use-ai-meeting-tools-responsibly/)
before using any feature that captures a conversation.
