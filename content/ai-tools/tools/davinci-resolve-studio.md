## Revisit the Original Before Cutting a Line

Blackmagic Design describes [transcription for editing](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/edit)
and [editing a timeline using transcribed text](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/media/partial/release/20240412-03).
Use a short fictional interview. If the speaker says, "The pilot worked
for our morning group, but we have not tested the evening group," check
whether your cut preserves which group was tested and which was not.
Listen before and after the edit point; the transcript is not the recording.

## Keep a Timecode for the Next Review

Keep the original clip, source time range, and reason for the cut. On a
later edit, compare the revised timeline with the same passage. The
[quote review practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-quote-against-a-transcript/)
offers fictional examples of checking context against a transcript.
Review [Studio pricing and features](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/studio)
and [privacy information](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/privacy)
before choosing a setup or sharing project media.
