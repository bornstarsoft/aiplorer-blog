## Overview

Shopify Sidekick is an AI assistant for commerce workflows that may help merchants prepare store content and business tasks for review. It may be useful for exploring how AI support could fit into store management, marketing, and operational planning.

This is a draft page. Official Shopify sources must be checked before publication, especially for current plans, pricing, limits, commerce capabilities, store access, permissions, data handling, security details, and availability.

## Useful For

Merchants may explore Sidekick for drafting store content, organizing commerce tasks, or preparing marketing and operational ideas for human review. Product facts, prices, claims, images, customer messages, and changes to a store should be checked before they are published or applied.

Test workflows before relying on them. Follow store permissions, consumer-protection requirements, privacy rules, brand guidance, media licenses, and any legal or compliance review that applies.

## Notes

Features, plans, pricing, limits, store access, permissions, and availability can change. Check official Shopify pages for current details.

Be careful with customer, order, product, payment-related, private, or confidential business data. Review generated content, automated actions, permissions, brand use, and security-sensitive changes before use.
