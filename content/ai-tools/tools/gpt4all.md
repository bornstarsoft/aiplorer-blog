## Where it fits

GPT4All is a Nomic desktop application for local language models. Its [official documentation](https://docs.gpt4all.io/) includes LocalDocs, a way to bring selected local documents into a chat workflow. Consider it when your starting point is a desktop app and a small reading task.

## A useful first trial

Use a folder containing a few non-sensitive documents with facts you can check. Ask one narrow question and inspect the cited or retrieved source material yourself. A locally produced answer can still omit context or invent a detail.

Download only a model that fits the current app and your machine. Check its license rather than assuming that all models share the application's terms.

## Before adding work documents

Inspect [settings](https://docs.gpt4all.io/gpt4all_desktop/settings.html), including optional sharing and remote connections. Do not grant broad file access simply because an app can run locally. Review [model guidance](https://docs.gpt4all.io/gpt4all_desktop/models.html) and the [maintainer repository](https://github.com/nomic-ai/gpt4all) for current compatibility and releases.
