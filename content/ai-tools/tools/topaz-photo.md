## Check What the Filter Did to Real Texture

The [Topaz Photo quick start](https://docs.topazlabs.com/topaz-photo/quick-start)
describes reducing noise, sharpening, and comparing the result in the
preview. Start with your own low-light photo of a fabric item with a
small label. Preview one adjustment. Check whether the fabric weave
still looks like the original, and whether the label or edges changed.

## Repeat the Check on Each New Export

Keep the source photo and record what was supposed to stay unchanged.
Review a revised export side by side at the same zoom and at the final
display size. The [image edit practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-image-edit-against-a-brief/)
shows why a clearer image can still fail a brief if a label has changed.

Check [pricing](https://www.topazlabs.com/pricing),
[cloud rendering guidance](https://docs.topazlabs.com/topaz-photo/cloud-rendering),
and [privacy information](https://www.topazlabs.com/privacy) before
choosing a setup or uploading images.
