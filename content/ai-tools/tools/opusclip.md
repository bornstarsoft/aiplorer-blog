## Try One Excerpt Without Posting It

The [OpusClip help center](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/introduction-to-opusclip)
describes turning existing recordings into shorter clips. Choose a recording
you own or are approved to share. Compare one suggested excerpt with the
lines just before and after it. Keep enough context for a new viewer to
understand what the speaker actually meant, especially if the clip quotes
an estimate, exception, or condition.

## Check the Captions and the Final Cut

Use the guides for [trimming](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/trim-extend-the-video)
and [caption correction](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/change-captions)
if those controls are available on your plan. Verify names, numbers, and
speaker changes; watch the export on the intended screen and make sure the
crop does not hide important text. The
[caption review practice pack](/use-cases/check-ai-captions-against-a-reference/)
lets you rehearse that check with fictional material.

Before making this a recurring process, recheck the [plan details](https://www.opus.pro/pricing),
[privacy policy](https://www.opus.pro/privacy-policy), and
[terms of service](https://www.opus.pro/terms-of-service). Confirm that
your consent and licenses cover the shorter version as well as the source.
