## Compare One Short Render With the Original

Topaz Video is the desktop enhancement app covered by the
[quick start](https://docs.topazlabs.com/topaz-video/quick-start).
Keep a copy of the original. Try noise reduction or upscaling on a short
section with motion and fine detail, then compare both videos at the same
display size. Pause on text and thin edges, and play the same section at
normal speed. An enhanced image is not evidence of what the camera
originally captured.

## Choose Where to Process the Footage

Check the [system requirements](https://docs.topazlabs.com/topaz-video/system-requirements)
and whether your intended workflow renders on your device or uses
[cloud rendering](https://docs.topazlabs.com/topaz-video/cloud-rendering).
Do not assume a desktop installation keeps every job local. Before a cloud
job, check your permission to upload the recording, the current
[privacy terms](https://www.topazlabs.com/privacy), and any processing costs.
If you already own Video AI, check the
[legacy product comparison](https://docs.topazlabs.com/topaz-video/tvai-vs-tv)
instead of assuming the same licensing or features apply.

Open the actual export in its destination app and check playback, timing,
and audio sync before processing the whole project. The
[export review practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-export-plan-against-delivery-requirements/)
shows how to compare a proposed export with a handoff brief; use it to
practice documenting your own video delivery checks.
