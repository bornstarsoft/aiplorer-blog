## Check What the Visual Counts

Napkin's [visual generation guide](https://help.napkin.ai/en/articles/15923699-visuals-generation)
describes making visual options from selected text. Try a fictional
recap: 20 people were invited, 12 attended, and 6 of the attendees
completed a feedback form. Check whether the visual distinguishes
the invited group from the people who attended. A graphic that says
"half of all invitees replied" is wrong even if it looks clear.

## Compare the Visual Each Time the Source Changes

Keep the source paragraph with the visual. When the figures change,
check the labels and denominators again using the
[slide chart practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-slide-chart-before-sharing/).
Open the actual output in the destination app before sharing. Check the
[export guide](https://help.napkin.ai/en/articles/15924383-visuals-export-png-svg-ppt-or-pdf),
[pricing](https://www.napkin.ai/pricing/), and
[privacy policy](https://www.napkin.ai/privacy-policy/) before using work
material.
