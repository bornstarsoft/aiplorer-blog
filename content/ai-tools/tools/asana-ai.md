## Separate Completed Work From Pending Approval

Asana's [Smart status guide](https://help.asana.com/s/article/smart-status?language=en)
describes drafting an update for a selected project and date range.
Use a fictional pilot where a checklist was completed but a stakeholder
has not approved the rollout. Verify that the draft keeps those facts
separate and does not invent an owner or a decision date.

## Carry Open Items Into the Next Update

Keep the reporting period, original task links, and open blockers.
When you revisit the project, check whether each blocker was resolved
or simply omitted. The
[meeting actions practice pack](/use-cases/check-ai-meeting-actions-against-decisions/)
helps you rehearse checking a claimed decision or owner against the source.

Before posting about real work, review current [pricing](https://asana.com/pricing),
[AI admin controls](https://help.asana.com/s/article/asana-ai-features-and-admin-controls?language=en_US),
and the [privacy statement](https://asana.com/terms/privacy-statement).
Check the audience and scope of the update yourself.
