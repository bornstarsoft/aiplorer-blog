## Check One Formula and One Row

Mathpix's [Snip PDF guide](https://mathpix.com/docs/snip/snip-with-pdfs)
describes converting a PDF into an editable note. Use a scanned sample
you created yourself, with an equation that uses a minus sign and a
subscript. Include a table with both a zero and a truly blank cell.
Compare those spots against the original before copying the result.

## Keep a Small Conversion Check

Record the page, any correction, and why it matters. Reuse that checklist
when the scan or edition changes. Try the
[data extraction practice pack](/use-cases/test-ai-data-extraction-with-missing-values/)
to rehearse keeping missing values separate from zeros. Before uploading
class or work material, check [Snip pricing](https://mathpix.com/pricing/snip),
the [privacy policy](https://mathpix.com/privacy), and your permission to
share the source.
