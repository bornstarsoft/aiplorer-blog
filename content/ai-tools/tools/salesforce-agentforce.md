## Useful For

Teams may explore Agentforce for planning support, sales, or operational workflow drafts. Any generated response, automated action, routing decision, or record change should be tested and reviewed before it affects a customer, employee, account, or production process.

Define human escalation and rollback steps before relying on automated workflows. Follow account permissions, workplace policy, privacy requirements, and applicable legal or compliance review.
