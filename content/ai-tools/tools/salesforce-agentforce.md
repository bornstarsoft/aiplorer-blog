## Overview

Salesforce Agentforce is an AI agent and business workflow product that may help teams prepare customer, employee, and operational workflows for review. It may be useful for organizations considering how AI-assisted actions could fit into existing business processes.

Salesforce's plans, pricing, limits, agent capabilities, connected-product access, permissions, data handling, security terms, and availability may change. Check official Salesforce pages before relying on a specific capability or policy.

## Useful For

Teams may explore Agentforce for planning support, sales, or operational workflow drafts. Any generated response, automated action, routing decision, or record change should be tested and reviewed before it affects a customer, employee, account, or production process.

Define human escalation and rollback steps before relying on automated workflows. Follow account permissions, workplace policy, privacy requirements, and applicable legal or compliance review.

## Notes

Features, plans, pricing, limits, connected-product behavior, permissions, and availability can change. Check official Salesforce pages for current details.

Be careful with customer, employee, account, private, or confidential business data. Review generated content, automated actions, permissions, and security-sensitive changes before use.
