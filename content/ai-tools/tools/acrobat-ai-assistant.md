## Read the Passage, Not Just the Answer

[Acrobat AI Assistant](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/generative-ai-pdf)
can answer questions about documents. Adobe's
[content usage FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/use-acrobat-ai/understand-usage-policies/user-disclosures.html)
explains that a citation can be missing or point to the wrong text. Try
a fictional workshop handout that says visitors must register, except
for people attending only the open house. Ask who needs to register.
Check the source passage for the exception before using the answer.

## Repeat the Check on the Revised PDF

Keep the question, document edition, and page reference. When the
handout changes, confirm the rule again rather than reusing the previous
answer. The [summary retest practice pack](/use-cases/retest-ai-summaries-with-a-reference-pack/)
offers a reference text and worksheet for comparing two runs.

Before using real documents, check [plans and pricing](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/pricing.html),
[privacy guidance](https://www.adobe.com/privacy.html), and your
organization's document sharing policy.
