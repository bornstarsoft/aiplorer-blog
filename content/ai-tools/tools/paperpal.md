## Keep the Finding Narrow

The [Paperpal help center](https://support.paperpal.com/support/solutions/articles/3000126441-how-does-paperpal-work-)
describes language suggestions for academic drafts. Try a fictional
abstract: "Among 18 volunteers at one library, the optional reading log
was associated with higher self-reported reading time." A clearer
revision should retain the sample size, the single site, the
self-reported measure, and the fact that this was an association,
not evidence that the log caused the change.

## Check the Claim Again After Each Revision

Put the edited sentence beside the original result and any cited
passage. Use the [citation review practice pack](/use-cases/check-whether-a-citation-supports-a-claim/)
to check whether a source supports the exact revised claim. Review
[pricing](https://paperpal.com/pricing) and [privacy](https://paperpal.com/privacy-policy),
and use fictional material unless you have permission to share the draft
and any underlying research data.
