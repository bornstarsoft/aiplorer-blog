## Try a New Layout on One Copy

The [Plus AI Remix guide](https://guide.plusai.com/ai-for-presentations/remix-slides)
describes reformatting an existing slide. Start with a copy of a
fictional proposal slide that says, "Request approval for a two-week
pilot; expansion would need a separate decision." Compare the original
and the reformatted version. Make sure the shorter slide does not turn
the request into an approval or make expansion sound automatic.

## Check the Brief Before Reusing the Slide

Keep the approved wording beside the new layout and note which changes
you accept. When you reuse a deck, recheck scope and audience, not just
spacing. The [slide outline practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-slide-outline-against-a-brief/)
offers examples of decisions that go beyond a brief.

Review [pricing](https://plusai.com/pricing),
[add-in and security information](https://plusai.com/security), and
[privacy](https://plusai.com/privacy-policy). Start with a fictional
deck if your organization's sharing rules are unclear.
