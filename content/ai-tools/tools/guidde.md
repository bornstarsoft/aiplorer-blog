## Match the Narration to the Screen

[Guidde Create](https://www.guidde.com/guidde-create) describes building
step-by-step videos from a captured workflow. The [speaker editor](https://help.guidde.com/en/articles/10200246-speaker-editor-edit-the-ai-generated-voiceover-or-record-upload-your-own)
lets authors review and edit AI narration. In a demo workspace, record
saving a fictional equipment request without submitting it. Verify that
the narration says "Save draft" when that button is visible, and that the
captions retain the distinction.

## Review a Revised Step With the Sound Off

After the interface changes, check the revised step against the current
screen with sound on and again with sound off. Use the
[caption review practice pack](/use-cases/check-ai-captions-against-a-reference/)
to rehearse catching incorrect labels and missing details. The
[video editor guide](https://help.guidde.com/en/articles/9790578-editing-guidde-videos)
explains the editing controls.

Review [pricing](https://www.guidde.com/pricing) and [privacy](https://www.guidde.com/privacy-policy)
before recording real workflows. Check for exposed notifications, account
details, or customer information in both the visuals and transcript.
