## Overview

Speechify is a voice AI productivity tool that may help users listen to text, dictate drafts, ask questions about content, and prepare spoken-audio workflows. It may be useful for reviewing a draft by ear, exploring a listening-based reading routine, or preparing voice-first notes and document work.

## Useful For

Users may explore Speechify for listening to text they are allowed to use, dictating a draft, asking questions about source material, reviewing pacing and pronunciation, or planning reading and study workflows. Spoken output, answers, summaries, notes, and podcast drafts should be checked against the original material before they are used for school, work, accessibility support, publishing, or public communication.
