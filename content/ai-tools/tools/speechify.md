## Overview

Speechify is a text-to-speech and reading tool that may help users listen to written material and prepare spoken-audio workflows. It may be useful for reviewing a draft by ear or exploring a listening-based reading routine.

Speechify plans, pricing, limits, voice options, supported content, downloads, accessibility details, licensing terms, privacy terms, security details, and availability may change. Check official Speechify pages before relying on a specific feature, right, or policy.

## Useful For

Users may explore Speechify for listening to text they are allowed to use, reviewing pacing and pronunciation, or planning reading and study workflows. Spoken output should be checked against the original text before it is used for school, work, accessibility support, publishing, or public communication.

Text-to-speech tools should not be treated as a guarantee that pronunciation, reading order, facts, document access, privacy, accessibility needs, or usage rights are correct.
