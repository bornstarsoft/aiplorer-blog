## Useful For

Users may explore Adobe Podcast for preparing a rough recording, planning spoken audio or optional video edits, reviewing enhanced speech, or checking a transcript. Enhancement results can vary with recording quality, speaker clarity, and background noise. Enhanced audio, transcripts, captions, edits, and factual content should be reviewed before publication or professional use.

Audio editing and enhancement tools should not be treated as a guarantee that speech is accurate, a recording is clear, people have consented, media is licensed, or output is private and ready for public or commercial use.
