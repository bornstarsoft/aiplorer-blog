## Overview

Descript is an AI video and audio editing platform that may help users edit by text, work with an AI co-editor, and prepare spoken-media drafts. It may be useful for creators, educators, marketers, and teams preparing video, podcast, narration, script, or caption drafts before final production.

Official Descript sources were reviewed for this page on 2026-07-26. Current plans, pricing, limits, editing and AI co-editor options, transcription or caption behavior, exports, integrations, voice options, third-party providers, privacy, security, licensing, commercial-use rules, and availability should still be checked before choosing or relying on the service.

## Useful For

Users may explore Descript-style workflows when planning an audio or video edit, preparing a spoken-media draft, reviewing scripts and captions, or asking an AI co-editor to prepare changes. The AI co-editor is in beta and may make incorrect assumptions or overpromise, so edited or generated video, audio, text, and captions should be checked before publishing, presenting, or using them in a real project.

Media editing tools should be treated as production support, not as a guarantee that facts are correct, speech is transcribed accurately, people have consented, media is licensed, brand rules are followed, or output is ready for public or commercial use.
