## Overview

Descript is an audio and video editing tool that may help users plan, draft, and review spoken-media workflows. It may be useful for creators, educators, marketers, and teams preparing video, podcast, narration, script, or caption drafts before final production.

## Useful For

Users may explore Descript-style workflows when planning an audio or video edit, preparing a spoken-media draft, or reviewing scripts and captions. Edited or generated video, audio, text, and captions should be reviewed before publishing, presenting, or using them in a real project.

Media editing tools should be treated as production support, not as a guarantee that facts are correct, speech is transcribed accurately, people have consented, media is licensed, brand rules are followed, or output is ready for public or commercial use.
