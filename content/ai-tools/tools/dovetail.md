## Follow One Theme Back to the Feedback

Dovetail's [Channels documentation](https://dovetail.com/product/ai-channels/)
describes grouping feedback. Its [Channels release notes](https://dovetail.com/changelog/channels-2-generally-available/)
show how grouped ideas link back to individual pieces of evidence. Start
with fictional comments that describe two different problems in similar
words. Inspect the original comments behind one generated theme. Make
sure the problem still means the same thing in context before sharing it.

## Recheck the Theme Before Changing Priorities

Record which source and time period you reviewed. On the next pass, check
for duplicate tickets, feedback from the same account, and a comment that
does not fit the theme. Do not treat a cluster of comments as a count of
unique customers or proof that the wider customer base agrees.

Before importing interviews or connecting live sources, review the
[pricing](https://dovetail.com/pricing/), [privacy policy](https://dovetail.com/privacy/privacy-policy/),
applicable [account terms](https://dovetail.com/legal/), and who can open
the original evidence. Use the [quote review practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-quote-against-a-transcript/)
to rehearse verifying an excerpt against its source before reuse.
