## Check the Words That Change the Instruction

CapCut's [caption guide](https://www.capcut.com/help/how-to-recognise-subtitles)
describes converting speech into captions and editing the result. Record
a fictional tutorial line such as, "Set the timer to fifteen seconds,
not fifty." Listen to the line while checking the caption for the
numbers, the word "not," and when each part appears.

## Check the Version Viewers Will See

Play the edited clip once with sound and once muted on a phone. If you
trim or crop the clip later, repeat the timing and readability checks.
The [caption review practice pack](/use-cases/check-ai-captions-against-a-reference/)
offers a reference script and a separate answer key.

Check current [pricing](https://www.capcut.com/pricing), the privacy
policy for your region (see [US privacy policy](https://www.capcut.com/clause/privacy-policy)),
and the rights to your footage, music, and other assets before sharing.
