## Compare the Label Before and After

Upscayl's [desktop download page](https://upscayl.org/download) describes
local processing. Try a small image you made with the fictional label
"Batch 04." Enlarge a copy, then check each character and the nearby
edges against the original. Extra pixels do not prove that a newly
visible detail was in the source.

## Keep a Sample for the Next Update

Keep the original, the desired size, and one reference export. If you
change models or versions, [repeat the same check](/use-cases/retest-a-saved-ai-tool-after-an-update/)
at the size viewers will see. Reject an enlargement that changes the
label, even if it looks sharper.

Check the [project's graphics requirements](https://github.com/upscayl/upscayl).
Use only images you have permission to process. If you choose Cloud
instead of Desktop, check its [pricing](https://upscayl.org/pricing) and
[privacy policy](https://upscayl.org/privacy) before uploading anything.
