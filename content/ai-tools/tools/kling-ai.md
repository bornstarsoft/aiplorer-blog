## What it is

Kling AI is Kuaishou's creative service for AI-generated images and video. Its official site includes text-to-video and image-to-video workflows. It is a candidate for exploring a scene or animating a visual reference before a human editor assembles the final work.

## A useful first trial

Use one image you have permission to upload. Describe a single action and camera movement, then check whether the subject remains consistent from beginning to end. Avoid starting with a complex multi-scene brief that makes errors difficult to isolate.

Compare the time needed to revise the result, not just the first preview. Confirm the selected model, costs, and output conditions inside the service before committing to repeated generation.
