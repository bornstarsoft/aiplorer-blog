## Model or app?

Veo is a Google video-generation model family, not a single standalone subscription. [Google DeepMind's overview](https://deepmind.google/models/veo/) explains the model; [Flow's getting-started guide](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353333?hl=en) explains one creative access route. Check the selected model inside the product rather than assuming every generated video uses Veo.

## A useful first trial

Write one shot with a subject, action, environment, and camera direction. Compare the output against that brief, including objects that change shape, movement between frames, and any generated audio. Treat the clip as material for review and editing, not evidence that an event occurred.

Before paying, confirm account and region eligibility, the available model, credit use, and output conditions. Do not assume access in one Google product transfers to another.

## Before sharing

Check consent for identifiable people, rights to reference media, and brand requirements. Review the service's current terms and [data FAQ](https://labs.google/fx/faq) before uploading private material.
