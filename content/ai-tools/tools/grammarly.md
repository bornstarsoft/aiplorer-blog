## Overview

Grammarly is a writing and communication assistant. It can be useful for people who want support with proofreading, drafting, rewriting, clarity, tone, grammar, and AI-assisted writing workflows.

Grammarly's current official pages describe writing suggestions, generative assistance, and agent-style tools, but plans, access, behavior, integrations, and policies may change. Users should check the official Grammarly website, plans page, support center, privacy information, and trust pages before relying on a specific feature.

## Useful For

Beginners may use Grammarly to review rough emails, documents, messages, or other drafts before sharing them. It can help users think through clarity, tone, style, and wording options, but suggestions should be checked before accepting them.

Writing feedback should be treated as a draft aid, not a replacement for human judgment. Users should make sure the final text, citations, source claims, detector results, and any agent-assisted output are accurate, appropriate for the audience, and suitable for the context.

## Notes

Features, plans, AI assistance, agent-style features, grammar-checking behavior, plagiarism or AI-detection features, enterprise or team availability, integrations, browser extension availability, app availability, model access, and regional availability may change. Check official Grammarly pages for current details before relying on a specific feature.

Do not enter sensitive or confidential text unless you understand the relevant privacy, product improvement and training settings, workplace, school, organization, and data handling policies. Grammarly suggestions and agent-style results should be treated as writing assistance, not as a guarantee that text is correct, properly sourced, appropriate, current, private, or safe to use without review.
