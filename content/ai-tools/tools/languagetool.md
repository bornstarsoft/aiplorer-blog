## Proofread Without Changing a Promise

The [LanguageTool editor guide](https://help.languagetool.org/hc/en-us/articles/39254517614999-What-is-the-LanguageTool-Editor)
describes grammar, spelling, and style suggestions. Start with a fictional
email that says, "We can review the draft on Tuesday, but we cannot
promise approval by Friday." Compare the original and the edited version.
Did the edit preserve who is doing the review, both dates, and the
difference between reviewing and approving?

## Recheck the Same Details on Your Next Draft

Keep a short list of names, numbers, deadlines, and commitments to check
each time you edit. The [rewrite practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-rewrite-without-changing-the-promise/)
provides sample text and a separate answer key for testing this habit.
Check the [supported languages](https://help.languagetool.org/hc/en-us/articles/39254526141463-What-languages-does-LanguageTool-support),
[pricing](https://languagetool.org/premium), and
[privacy policy](https://languagetool.org/legal/privacy) before choosing
an editing mode or submitting work text.
