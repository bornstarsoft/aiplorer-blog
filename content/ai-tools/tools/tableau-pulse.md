## Check the Definition Before Comparing Two Weeks

Tableau's [metric guide](https://help.tableau.com/current/online/en-us/pulse_explore_metrics.htm)
describes following metrics and reading their digests. Tableau's
[site setup guide](https://help.tableau.com/current/online/en-us/pulse_set_up.htm)
explains that optional AI summaries put insights into words. In a sample
completion rate, check whether canceled requests were excluded in both
weeks. A higher rate is not directly comparable if the denominator changed.

## Keep a Short Record for the Next Digest

Write down the definition, period, filters, and any source change.
Revisit that record before repeating a claim, and use the
[slide chart practice pack](/use-cases/check-an-ai-slide-chart-before-sharing/)
to rehearse distinguishing denominators and observed changes. Check
[pricing](https://www.tableau.com/pricing), site settings, sharing
permissions, and [AI data usage and privacy](https://help.tableau.com/current/tableau/en-us/tableau_gai_data.htm)
before following sensitive metrics.
