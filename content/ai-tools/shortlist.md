Your shortlist is stored only in this browser. Aiplorer does not receive or
sync the saved list, and clearing browser storage may remove it.

Each saved card shows one reviewed task-fit example, one check-first note, and a
four-part candidate checklist. The checks are private browser notes, not scores,
rankings, or recommendations.

Use those signals to narrow the list, then open each full review and official
website before choosing. They are not complete risk assessments. Features,
pricing, limits, policies, and availability can change after an Aiplorer review.
